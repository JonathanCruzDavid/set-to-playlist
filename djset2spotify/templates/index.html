Mix it in Spotify

Use Spotify's built-in Mix button for DJ-style transitions. You need Spotify Premium; no other apps are required.

  1. Open the playlist in Spotify and click Mix (next to + Add). Spotify's AI sets an Auto transition between every song. Click Save; you can already press play and hear a full mix.
  2. Find the rough transitions. Click an Auto label to open Edit transition, then click ■ in the waveform to preview it. Watch for two problems:
    • Big tempo jumps: more than about 5 BPM between the two songs (e.g. 138 → 128).
    • Key clashes: the key badges (e.g. 8A → 4B) aren't neighbours. Neighbours share a number (8A ↔ 8B) or sit one number apart with the same letter (8A ↔ 7A / 9A).
  3. Fix only those by choosing a preset, then set the length using the bars button under the waveform:
    If the two songs…PresetLength
    Are close in tempo and the keys matchBlend16 bars
    Step up in energy or tempo, keys OKRise8 bars
    Have a big tempo jump or clashing keysFade4 bars
    Already sound goodAutoleave it
  4. Click Save after editing, then play from track 1.
